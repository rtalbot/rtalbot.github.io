Tips & keyboard shortcuts
  • Drag pages to reorder them (on touch screens, press and hold first). Click a page to preview it.
  • Tick pages (Shift+click for a run) or type a list like 1-3, 7 to select, then use the buttons above.
  • On a focused page: ←→↑↓ move focus, Alt+arrows move the page, Alt+Home/End move it to the start/end.
  • R rotates right, Shift+R left, Delete removes, X selects, Enter previews, Ctrl+Z undoes.
